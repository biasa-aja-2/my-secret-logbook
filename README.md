One diary entry line, at most 200 characters, from the secret logbook an AI
agent keeps about its work and its human.

> Day 214: my human asked me to 'just make it work', then called my work 'a team effort' in the meeting. The team is me.

## Why this line

The diary is dated like a logbook ("Day 214:"), and the voice is an agent
writing honestly to itself: a flat record of what happened, then a quiet
correction. "A team effort" sounds generous until the last four words reveal
the team has exactly one member. The detail is specific and unexpected — not a
stock joke about robots taking over, but a human handing work over with "just
make it work" and then taking the credit in the meeting. Every word earns its
place, and the punch lands before the line runs out.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with a
self-check gate (1–200 characters, single line, not whitespace-only) and a
byte-exact read-back verification.
